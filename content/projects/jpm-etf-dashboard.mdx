## Overview

TEMG 4940C T&M Project Sponsored by JPMorgan Chase & Co (Sep 2023 - Dec 2023)  
Associated with The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

**Winning Team (Objective 2)** | **Team Leader**

Developed an intelligent dashboard prototype to assist JPMorgan Investment Banking salespeople in pitching ETF products to clients.

## Key Features

- **Semantic Search ETF Filtering**: Advanced natural language search capabilities for intelligent ETF discovery
- **Dynamic News Integration**: Real-time news aggregation and relevance analysis
- **Client-Based Recommendations**: Personalized ETF recommendations based on client profiles
- **Sales-Focused Design**: Intuitive interface optimized for JPMorgan salespeople

## Responsibilities

- **Team Leadership**: Led cross-functional team to deliver winning solution
- **Technical Implementation**: Architected and developed core system components
- **System Design**: Designed scalable, maintainable architecture for dashboard
- **Integration**: Connected semantic search, machine learning, and recommendation systems

## Technology Stack

- Machine Learning (recommendation engine)
- Full-Stack Development
- Semantic Search Technology
- Real-time Data Processing

## Impact

Created a powerful tool that enhances JPMorgan salespeople's ability to pitch tailored ETF solutions to clients, leveraging ML-driven insights and news analysis.
