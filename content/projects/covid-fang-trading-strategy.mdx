## Overview

COMP 4971C Independent Study Course (Sep 2021 - Dec 2021)  
Associated with The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

Developed a data-driven trading strategy analyzing the influence of COVID-19 pandemic on FANG+ technology company stock valuations using machine learning and natural language processing.

## Methodology

### Data Collection
- **CDC COVID-19 Statistics**: Aggregated official COVID-19 case and death data
- **Reddit Sentiment Data**: Collected Reddit posts discussing pandemic impacts
- **Market Data**: FANG+ stock price and valuation information

### NLP Analysis
- **Semantic Processing**: Applied open-source NLP tools for semantic analysis
- **Sentiment Extraction**: Analyzed Reddit post titles to extract sentiment signals about pandemic concerns
- **Trend Identification**: Identified correlations between public sentiment and market movements

### Predictive Modeling
- **Time Series Forecasting**: Developed TensorFlow-based models to predict future stock valuations
- **Feature Integration**: Combined COVID statistics and sentiment data as model inputs
- **Validation**: Backtested strategy performance against actual market outcomes

## Key Components

1. **Data Pipeline**: Python-based ETL pipeline for collecting and processing data
2. **NLP Engine**: Semantic analysis of social media and news sentiment
3. **ML Model**: TensorFlow time series model incorporating multi-source data
4. **Strategy Evaluation**: Performance metrics and risk assessment

## Technology Stack

- **Python**: Data collection and processing
- **NLP**: Open-source semantic analysis tools
- **Machine Learning**: TensorFlow for time series forecasting
- **Data Analysis**: Pandas, NumPy for statistical analysis

## Insights

The project demonstrated the relationship between pandemic sentiment (measured via social media and health statistics) and FANG+ stock performance, providing a data-driven framework for trading strategy development during periods of significant external shock.
